    Frequently Asked Questions

    When does HGA reopen?

    Booking opens January 18, 2027, and grading begins February 1, 2027.

    What happens after I purchase?

    From January 18, we email you an invite to book your grading date on the HGA Grading Calendar. Then send your cards any time before your date.

    How does tracking work?

    Your card's progress is tracked through U-Collect as it moves through each step: received, inspected, graded, protected and revealed. Your delivery-to-reveal clock starts the moment the carrier delivers your card.

    What information is in the report?

    Your final grade, all four subgrades (centering, corners, edges and surface) and a report explaining your grading results.

    How is a card verified?

    HGA's protective casing includes an RFID chip that connects your graded card to its digital verification information through U-Collect.

    Which cards qualify?

    We grade all sports and all TCG. Presale passes cover standard size cards. Booklets, folds and decks are booked separately.

    Can I transfer my purchase?

    No. Presale purchases can't be transferred.
