FAQs

What's different about the new HGA?

HGA's new system lets you visually follow your card from delivery to grade reveal, with U-Collect tracking, a guaranteed delivery-to-reveal clock, FREE subgrades and a full grading report explaining your final grade.

When does the 30-business-day clock start?

Your guaranteed delivery-to-reveal clock starts the moment the carrier delivers your card to HGA. You'll see your final grade, subgrades and grading report through your U-Collect portal within 30 business days, guaranteed.

What's the difference between Premium Aerospace Glass and HGA Protective Poly?

Premium Aerospace Glass ($200 pre-launch) includes everything in Poly, plus premium aerospace engineered glass + metal housing and a museum-worthy presentation for your most prized collectibles. HGA Protective Poly ($29 pre-launch) gets your card professionally graded, protected and verified.

How does the Priority Grading Calendar work?

Select an available grading date, ship your cards ahead of time, and watch them get graded.

Is there an upcharge for high-value cards?

There's no card-value upcharge on cards valued under $10,000.

How does RFID verification work?

Tap your card's label with your phone to access its permanent digital record. Each card is also blockchain-certified, adding a layer of protection against card swapping and fraudulent verification.

What card sizes can HGA grade?

HGA accommodates over 60 different card sizes, from standard trading cards to oversized collectibles and unique booklet formats. If we don't have a size that fits your card, we'll create one that does.

What card categories can I submit?

From sports and TCGs to entertainment and pop culture, including Sports, Pokemon, Yu-Gi-Oh, Magic, UFC and Disney cards.
